Our Story

Why we exist

The International Center of Evangelism (ICE) exists to do everything in its power to help the Church of Jesus Christ complete His Great Commission within this generation.

ICE is a ministry of Evangelism Resources, founded to equip national (overseas) churches for evangelism by Dr. & Mrs. Willys Braun in Kinshasa, DRC. Today it serves churches across Nigeria and neighbouring nations.

  1. 1991The English-language School of Evangelism moves to Lagos, Nigeria, then a couple of years later to Owerri.
  2. 1994Drs. Mawo and Janet Abaya join ER to lead the International Center of Evangelism in Nigeria.
  3. 1999The School of Evangelism moves to rented facilities in Jos.
  4. 2011ICE settles into its present permanent facility, GILGAL, in Jos.

Statement of Faith

What we believe

Everything we do rests on these convictions. The work of world evangelization is the supreme task of the Church.

God

The eternal Creator, one in nature and power, in three persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit. Omnipotent, omniscient and omnipresent.

Jesus Christ

Son of God and Son of man, born of the virgin Mary. He bore the sins of all men on the cross, rose on the third day, ascended, and will come again to receive His Church and judge the living and the dead.

The Holy Spirit

The third Person of the Trinity, given to every believer. He bears witness that we are children of God, cleanses from sin, empowers for service and bears fruit in our lives.

The Bible

The holy, inspired and inerrant Word of God: the 39 books of the Old Testament and 27 of the New, wholly without error in their original form.

Man

The fallen object of God’s perfect love, lost through sin but able to accept His free grace. All need a Savior, and Christ alone is that Savior.

Justification & Sanctification by Faith

No one comes to the Father but by the Son, and none comes apart from true repentance and faith.

Holiness

The norm for the Christian life, available to every believer through the indwelling fullness of the Holy Spirit.

The Church

The body of Christ, washed by His blood. It is His plan for the Church to evangelise the world before He comes again.
